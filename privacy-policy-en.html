2. About the App

Vizyon+ (MovieExplorer): Discovery of movies, TV series and anime; trailers and watch links; favourites, watchlist, follow and reminder features.

3. Data We Collect

  • Account data (only if the user registers/signs in): email address, display name; passwords are not stored in plain text (salted SHA-256).
  • User content (stored on-device): favourites, watchlist, ratings, watched records, followed titles, reminders, usage history (Room database).
  • Session data: "stay signed in" preference and session key (DataStore).
  • Google Sign-In: the Google account email and name the user approves.
  • Advertising data: advertising ID and device/usage data processed by AdMob.
  • Content data (third party): movie/TV/anime information and images shown via the TMDB API.
  • WebView data: temporary cookies/session data of sites visited inside the in-app browser.

4. Purposes

  • Account creation and session management.
  • Providing favourites, watchlist, rating, watched, follow and reminder features.
  • Content discovery and recommendations (using TMDB data).
  • Showing ads (personalised or non-personalised based on consent).
  • Notifications (reminders, follows, suggestions).
  • Legal obligations and defence in disputes.

5. Legal Bases

  • Consent (ads, optional data).
  • Performance of a contract (account and list features).
  • Legal obligation.
  • Legitimate interest (core operation of the app) where applicable.

6. Service Providers and Transfers

  • TMDB (The Movie Database): content info and images; subject to TMDB terms. Attribution: "This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB."
  • Google (AdMob): ad serving; subject to Google Ads policies.
  • Google Sign-In: account info upon user approval.
  • YouTube: trailer links (referral only).
  • International transfers: TMDB, Google/AdMob and YouTube servers may be abroad.

7. Advertising and Personalised Ads

Personalised ads are only shown with the user's consent. Users can change consent in "Ads/Privacy Preferences". More info: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

8. Cookies / WebView

Sites visited in the in-app browser (WebView) may use their own cookies. The app does not control those cookies; browsing history is not stored.

9. Retention

  • Local data stays on the device until the account is deleted or the app is removed.
  • Account deletion permanently removes local user data.
  • Third-party data is governed by their own policies.
